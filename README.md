# Arc Ads Notes

Arc ads is how most people search for Arcads, a studio that builds video ads from AI actors. Good at production, silent about what to produce.

**Read the full page:** https://arc-ads.github.io/

As a production tool this is strong: a library the site describes as more than a thousand AI actors, the option to create your own avatar, and a studio stacked with editing utilities from captions to actor swapping to translation. Performance marketers who currently pay for footage every time they want to test a hook will feel the difference immediately. What it does not do is tell you which hook to test, which competitor is already winning with it, or which creator to brief next, and that is the gap Reachara fills. Also note the promotional banner: limited-time unlimited generation offers mean the value of a subscription changes depending on the week you sign up.
